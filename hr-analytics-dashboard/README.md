# HR Analytics Dashboard

**Tool:** Power BI | **Type:** Professional training course project

## Description
Built an HR Analytics dashboard using a sample employee dataset. The dashboard tracks employee attrition and key HR metrics such as headcount, average age, tenure, and salary, with visuals to break down attrition by department, gender, education, salary, age group, and job role.

## Features
- KPI cards for headcount, attrition count/rate, average age, tenure, and salary
- Treemap of attrition by gender
- Donut chart of attrition by education level
- Bar and column charts of attrition by salary band and age group
- Area chart of attrition by years at company
- Pivot table of attrition by job role vs. job satisfaction
- Department slicer for interactive filtering

## Skills Demonstrated
Data modeling (fact/lookup tables), DAX measures, KPI card design, categorical & trend visualizations, interactive filtering
